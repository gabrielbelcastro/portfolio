Miles Terminal

Web app · AI reading · Labs Aero

A web terminal for people who run miles operations: in one place it processes LATAM cards and statements, shows each agent's performance from the sales spreadsheet and builds a multi-airline flight search from a screenshot, read by AI. It grew out of my own desk and was used by agency ticketing agents with individual logins.

Three modules, three jobs that used to be manual

Per-agent metrics, no server

Performance module

Reads the sales spreadsheet (a file or a live Google Sheets link, refreshed every 15 minutes) and shows each person's tickets, revenue, profit and margin. Everything is computed in the browser; the data never reaches the server.

LATAM cards and statements

CUV/Extracts module

Opens the booking, works out sale, cost, fees and commission from the price per thousand miles, and pulls data from the statement PDF to check the ticket.

Flight search from a screenshot

Search module

The agent pastes the itinerary screenshot the customer sent; AI reads origin, destination and dates, and the terminal builds the ready search on Smiles, LATAM, Azul and American.

Screenshots of the screens with no data, taken from the project's code. The terminal requires an authorized Google login, so there is no open demo.
